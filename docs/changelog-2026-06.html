Changelog

June 2026

v0.1.0 — first release
🖥️

A terminal emulator in pure MFL

The first release of machin-terminal: a minimal, fast, single-window terminal emulator written entirely in the machin language. It opens a window, runs your shell, and renders a live character grid at 60fps — all from one ~1.1 MB static binary with no GTK, VTE, Python, or Node.

🧰

Runs your full-screen tools

Verified hosting vim, htop, and tmux. The alt-screen buffer means full-screen apps never clobber your scrollback, scroll regions and reverse-video status bars render correctly, and resizing the window reflows and notifies the shell.

🎨

Color, Unicode & box-drawing

Full SGR color support — 16, 256, and 24-bit truecolor — plus reverse video. A UTF-8 decoder with box-drawing, block, arrow, and symbol glyph coverage means tmux pane borders and Unicode output render as crisp lines, not garbage.

🪶

tmux is the multiplexer

No native tabs or splits — by design. tmux already does multiplexing better, and runs inside machin-terminal flawlessly. The emulator stays small and correct, doing one thing well: being a great pane of glass.
